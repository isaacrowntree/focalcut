How this page works
Times are in each export file's own timeline. Scrub source opens the export the shot comes from: the
yellow block is this shot, grey blocks are the reel's other shots from that
video, blue ticks are the moments the planner found. Mark extra shots anywhere
with M (as many as you like), swap this shot to the playhead or an I/O range
(S), ban it (B), or ask for more from the video. Drag either strip to scrub; the lower one is
zoomed around the playhead for frame-accurate picks (scroll or =/− to zoom); J/K/L shuttle. Then save the
picks as focalcut/reel/picks.json and re-run
--steps plan,render,xml,review.
